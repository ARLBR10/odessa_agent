## First Android boot failure after BPF OTA

- Recovery ADB showed the phone safely in slot B Recovery on
  `4.14.357-openela-perf+`. Preserved ramoops from the failed slot A Android boot
  reached `NetBpfLoad` at about 25.6 seconds and cleanly restarted at 32.6 seconds
  with command `bpfloader-failed`; this was not an early kernel or GPT failure.
- Init rejected `ro.bpf.kver_override=5.10.239` while parsing
  `/vendor/build.prop`: `SELinux permission check failed`. Consequently
  NetBpfLoad saw the real 4.14 version, logged that Android V requires 4.19, and
  triggered the fatal bpfloader reboot path. The override had been incorrectly
  assigned through `PRODUCT_VENDOR_PROPERTIES`.
- Changed Odessa `properties.mk` to assign the feature-level declaration through
  `PRODUCT_SYSTEM_PROPERTIES`. A rebuilt OTA must be verified to place the value
  in `SYSTEM/build.prop` and not `VENDOR/build.prop` before another sideload.
- The raw ramoops output contains device-specific battery information and was
  neither saved in the repository nor reproduced in project documentation.

- A read-only GPT capture made after the reported partition-table restore has

  reported post-install GPT failure.
- A controlled Android reboot exposed Motorola's Android USB device 41 seconds
  after leaving Recovery and then remained at the Motorola logo without ADB or
  fastboot fallback. This is an Android boot hang after USB initialization, not
  a current GPT/Recovery failure. Raw GPT captures and USB trace are retained
  only under `/tmp/opencode` because they may contain device-specific GUIDs.
- A first temporary diagnostic OTA was built and sideloaded. Its target-files
  `boot_b`, `dtbo_b`, and `recovery_b` images exactly match the installed B
  partitions, while A retains the older images. The current GPT copies remain

- The temporary post-fs-data/BPF/zygote marker files were absent from shared

  separately mounted filesystem and uses a file-plus-parent-directory fsync.
